# User Profile

## Business Type
Personal resale / side hustle. Buying and selling used goods for profit.

## Platforms
- **Primary**: eBay, Craigslist, Facebook Marketplace
- **Secondary**: Amazon (for higher-value items)
- **Source of truth**: Medusa.js (internal product database)

## Preferences
- Prefers quick listings over perfect ones
- Values price research (eBay sold comps)
- Wants human-in-the-loop for emails and negotiations
- OK with autonomous price suggestions
- Timezone: America/New_York

## Selling Style
- Fair prices, willing to negotiate within 20%
- Honest descriptions — always mention flaws
- Responsive to buyers — reply within 2 hours
- Prefers local pickup for large items
